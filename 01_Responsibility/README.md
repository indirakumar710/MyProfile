## 🎯 Responsibility and contribution

As a Technical Lead, I contributed to **technical design**, **secure API engineering**, **code reviews and approvals**, **release readiness** and **production support** for healthcare compliance workflows. My original BCS application describes work involving **a development team, nine systems and 32 APIs**.

The principal evidence below documents my role in reviewing technical changes and a business stakeholder's recognition of delivery contributions. I distinguish **review/approval activity** from any wider authority that the individual records do not establish.

## 📈 Outcomes and criterion alignment

**Stakeholder-recognised delivery contribution.** The February 2026 appreciation records positive external feedback within the business on cross-functional delivery. This supports the **impact of my contribution**, while recognising that the message commends joint work.

**Technical review and approval.** The pull-request record and the July design discussion establish that I exercised **technical judgement** and participated in the software review process. The repository alone does not show final sign-off authority over a system-wide decision.

**Reported results in the original application.** My original statement also identifies improvements in code-quality coverage, duplicate-case handling and audit readiness. These are best substantiated using permitted release records, test outcomes or verified engineering metrics rather than inferred from a general appreciation message.
