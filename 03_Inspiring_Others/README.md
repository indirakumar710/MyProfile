## My contribution to developing other engineers

As a Technical Lead, I shared practical guidance through **code reviews**, **design discussions**, **mentoring** and **technical knowledge-sharing**. My reported activity includes mentoring **25+ engineers** and delivering **35+ hours of technical training annually**; those aggregate figures should be supported by training records if relied upon as verified counts.

The central evidence is a **specific, dated example of another engineer acting on my advice**, rather than a general claim that mentoring took place.

## 🔎 Case study — recommendation leading to a revised implementation

| **Stage** | **What occurred** | **Evidence** |

| **My recommendation** | On **26 February 2026**, I questioned the fixed value and recommended using the **appropriate number-of-days parameter**. | My dated review comment. |
| **Action by another engineer** | The developer stated that the requested changes had been made. | Reply in the same review discussion. |
| **After** | The visible code comparison used a **parameter-based value** instead of the hard-coded constant. | Revised implementation shown in screenshot. |
| **Outcome shown** | My technical guidance led to an **acknowledged implementation revision**. | Review thread and code comparison. |

**Evidence boundary:** The screenshot does **not** independently prove final merge, production deployment or that this change became a wider team standard.

Other engineers explained that the functionality was already being reused. This demonstrates **constructive engineering dialogue**, but it would be incorrect to claim I initiated reuse across the team.

**Public education:** My [September 2025 developer meetup](../04_Public_Influencer/README.md) and [August 2026 software architecture conference](../04_Public_Influencer/README.md) further demonstrate knowledge-sharing beyond the employer. Their main evidence belongs under **Public Influencer**.

### How this aligns with the criterion

**Demonstrated:** Another professional responded to my technical guidance and revised an implementation. A manager also independently confirmed ongoing mentorship and explanation of engineering concepts.

**Further substantiation that would strengthen a practice-change claim:** a merged pull request, another developer applying the same approach, a dated team standard, or a firsthand statement describing sustained adoption.

> **Confidentiality:** Internal Bitbucket screenshots and private signed references should not be available publicly without consent and authorisation. Use an approved redacted exhibit or a private BCS evidence channel.
